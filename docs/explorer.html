Simulation-based calibration

A flat histogram inside the shaded band means the posteriors are calibrated. A U shape means overconfident, an arch means under-confident, a tilt means biased. Triangles mark where the selected simulation's truth ranks. Red χ̂² values fall outside the 1σ tolerance.

Simulation

Contours enclose 68% and 95% of each posterior, drawn from 1,000 samples of the filtered chain (thin 11, Δlog p = 10), so they are slightly noisier than the published figures. Ranks use the full filtered chain and reproduce the published calibration ratios.
